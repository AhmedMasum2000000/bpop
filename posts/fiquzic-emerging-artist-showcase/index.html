Fiquzic: An Emerging Star on Our Radar

  • VIEW POST ON INSTAGRAM ↗

Get2Know Tags presents an exclusive showcase on Fiquzic, a new name B-POP Tags has drafted onto its radar ahead of the algorithm.

Fiquzic is the emerging artist at the centre of this showcase, presented by @get2know.tags and shared by B-POP Tags on 20 October 2026. The caption calls @fiquzic "an emerging star drafted on our Radar" and asks followers to "keep this between us until the algorithm catches up on him."

An emerging artist showcase, not a press release

The post doesn't come with a biography, a release list or tour dates, and we won't make any up. What it offers is a signal: this is someone we think is worth hearing early. The cover puts the B-POP Tags logo beside the "Discover Fresh" and Get2Know Tags marks, over an illustrated portrait framed by a checkerboard band, with a line about "tomorrow's headliners" running across the top.

Following Fiquzic from the start

The showcase follows our Early Access post on Fiquzic from the day before, which promised an exclusive interview on Get2Know Tags. Together the two posts show how B-POP approaches music in Bangladesh: spotting artists before the wider feeds do. If you like finding names early, try our pieces on Mass $cott and Rodoshi's "Arektu".

The caption signs off with a question, "WHO'S NEXT???", so tell us who belongs on the radar. More discoveries are on the B-POP Tags home page.
