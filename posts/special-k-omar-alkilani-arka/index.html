Special K x Omar Alkilani: The Magic Produced by Arka

স্পেশাল কে ও ওমর আলকিলানি: আরকার প্রযোজনায় এক জাদুকরী পারফরম্যান্স

Some collaborations only need a press of play. B-POP Tags shares a performance by Special K and Omar Alkilani, with full credit to its creators.

Some collaborations need no explanation, just a press of play, and Special K x Omar Alkilani is one of them. This post spotlights a performance created by Special K (@specialk.music) and Omar Alkilani (@omaralkilaniofficial), produced by @arkamag.co, @arkacollective.co and @hijazconvoy.

Special K x Omar Alkilani, produced by Arka

B-POP Tags is upfront about it: "all the credits go to the respective creators", and the cover carries a clear "Video courtesy: Arka Mag IG" line. The video still shows two musicians performing in a grand courtyard lined with arches and columns. One plays a keyboard setup fronted by a glowing "arka" sign, the other a stringed instrument, with smoke drifting across the floor and speakers set on a patterned rug. The Hijaz Convoy logo sits in the top corner.

Where the tags point

The hashtags pair #popmusic and #arabmusic with #bangladeshi, #popculture and #arka, the same tag we used for our post on Samaha Subah.

For more artists reaching beyond borders, read about Halfcian of Salted Jus, or dive into our B-POP x IIZXXZII Spotify playlist. There's more in Music & Rave and on the B-POP Tags home page.
