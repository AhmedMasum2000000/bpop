The Takeover of Qinetic Music: The Label Rewriting Bengali Pop

কিনেটিক মিউজিকের দখল: যে লেবেল নতুন করে লিখছে বাংলা পপ

  • VIEW POST ON INSTAGRAM ↗
  • ← HOME

Swipe to see how one indie label rewrote the DNA of Bengali pop: B-POP Tags on the takeover of Qinetic Music.

Qinetic Music is, in B-POP Tags' words, the indie label that "completely rewrote the DNA of Bengali Pop." Our 7 September 2026 post called it "the insane takeover of Qinetic Music", and the cover put it even more plainly: "The label behind Bangla pop's global take over."

Qinetic Music and the new Bangla pop

The post is a swipe-through carousel, and it makes its case slide by slide on Instagram. What the caption makes clear is the framing. This is an independent label rather than a major, and in B-POP's view it has changed what Bengali pop sounds like and how far it travels.

The cover leans into that confidence. A glossy blue "Q" with white angel wings and a golden halo floats over a dark sci-fi frame, with "SWIPE" in the corner.

Who the post tagged

Alongside #QineticMusic, #BanglaPop and #BengaliMusic, the post tagged @qineticmusic, @qineticnetwork, @mcfaf.a, @sharafnazaar and @jamshedps. Follow them for the label's latest releases.

More on Bengali pop

For artists taking Bangladeshi music worldwide, read about Halfcian of Salted Jus. For new listening, try the B-POP x IIZXXZII playlist and Rodoshi's "Arektu". More is on our music hub and the B-POP Tags home page.
