Off the grid but definitely on our radar: B-POP Tags' preview of Dhaka events for early October 2026.

Dhaka events, October 2026: these are the ones that sat off the grid but on our radar. Looking for nights that don't show up on the usual listings? On 2 October B-POP Tags posted a preview under a headline that sums up the idea: "Off the grid but definitely on our radar." The caption asked followers which one they were excited for.

How our Dhaka events preview for October 2026 works

Our weekly roundups look back. This format looks ahead. It gathers upcoming events from the edges of the scene, the kind you often only hear about from a friend or a story that disappears in a day. The picks themselves are on the slides of the original post, so swipe through there for names, dates and places.

The cover sets the mood in neon green: the "Off the grid / on our radar" line, the B-POP Tags star, a giant "DHAKA" and two anime-style characters in headphones, one pair glowing with a star on the ear cup.
