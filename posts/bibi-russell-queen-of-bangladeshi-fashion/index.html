Bibi Russell: The Queen of Bangladesh's Fashion Industry

বিবি রাসেল: বাংলাদেশের ফ্যাশন ডিজাইন জগতের রানি

Bengali pop culture wasn't built in a day. B-POP Tags honours Bibi Russell, the queen of the country's fashion design industry.

Bengali pop culture "wasn't built in a day". B-POP Tags opened its tribute to Bibi Russell with that reminder: innovators came first and "paved the path for development and global recognition".

Bibi Russell, queen of Bangladeshi fashion design

The post calls @bibi_russell_official "the queen of our fashion designing industry" and says she "deserves all the flowers for her work and dedication". It also tags two related accounts, @bibirussellshop and @bibirussell_dhaniakhali, for anyone who wants to explore further.

A tribute in pink and blue

The cover is bold and simple: her name in huge hot-pink capitals on black, above two blue-tinted portraits. The hashtags, #bibirussell, #fashiondesigners, #bangladeshidesigner and #popculturebd, place her where B-POP believes she belongs, at the root of the culture.

Why we look back

Celebrating the people who laid the foundations is part of how we cover today's scene in Art & Fashion. Read how Samaha Subah helps Bangladeshi heritage set international trends, see where fashion is heading with cybersigilism, and meet another pioneer in Jamil's Comics and Bangladesh's comic book culture.
