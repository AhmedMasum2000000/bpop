Which one did you miss last week, and which one did we fail to capture? The B-POP radar for 22 September 2026.

The Dhaka scene roundup for 22 September 2026 came with two questions. The first is the series staple: "Which one did you miss last week?" The second turns to us: readers were asked to comment on "which one we failed to capture on our radar this week."

What's in the 22 September Dhaka scene roundup

The events themselves are on the slides of the original Instagram post. The cover is in the series' style, with the B-POP Tags logo over a warm brown gradient, "DHAKA" in giant glowing letters and an illustrated woman in orange tones with her face turned to the sky, apartment blocks below.

A radar that admits its blind spots

Asking what we missed is deliberate. Dhaka's independent scene runs on word of mouth, and a lot happens in rooms that never make it onto a poster. The comment section works as a correction log: every name someone adds is a crew or venue we can follow from then on.

Two days earlier we posted Dhaka events to save for September 2026, so this roundup is partly its follow-up. For neighbouring weeks, see the 9 September roundup and the 6 October roundup. All of them sit on our events and radar hub, and the rest of the magazine is on the B-POP Tags home page.
