Learn From the Best: Omnigroove Collective DJ Workshop

অমনিগ্রুভ কালেক্টিভের ডিজে ওয়ার্কশপ: সেরাদের কাছ থেকে শিখুন

  • VIEW POST ON INSTAGRAM ↗
  • ← HOME

Omnigroove Collective is opening the booth to beginners, with three artists it calls Bangladesh's finest leading the way.

Ever wanted to learn DJing from people already playing out? A DJ workshop in Bangladesh presented by @omnigroove.collective promises exactly that, with a teaching line-up the collective describes as "Bangladesh's finest".

Who's teaching this DJ workshop in Bangladesh

The post names three artists:

  • Psytaraa (@psytaraa.artist), credited alongside @deltatribe.bd
  • @vampbetch_
  • @momi_hoque

Psytaraa's name runs across the top of the cover in bold italic type, set against a swirling teal-and-grey shape. Below it is a collage: a figure in sunglasses lit in electric blue, a psychedelic eye illustration, and a man with headphones around his neck.

Learn the art of DJing

The caption's pitch is short: "Learn the art of DJing", and don't miss the opportunity. The hashtags (#djculture, #bangladeshidj, #raveculture, #psytara) put the workshop firmly on the rave side of the scene. The post doesn't list a date, venue, fee or number of places, so follow Omnigroove Collective for sign-up details.

Building skills in the scene

DJ skills are often passed on informally, from one selector to the next, so a workshop opens that knowledge to anyone curious enough to show up. If you're building toward your first sets, also look at the Hong Kong radio show opportunity for Bangladeshi DJs and producers and read how OMDG helped spark Bangladesh's rave revolution. Fans of psychedelic culture should meet Lunith.Void too.

There's more in Music & Rave and on the B-POP Tags home page.
