Blackzang: The King Who Redefined Bangladeshi Hip Hop

ব্ল্যাকজ্যাং: বাংলাদেশের হিপহপকে নতুন সংজ্ঞা দেওয়া কিং

পড়ুন

  • VIEW POST ON INSTAGRAM ↗
  • ← HOME

A crown, a portrait and a simple message: B-POP Tags celebrates Blackzang, the king who redefined Bangladeshi hip hop.

Some tributes don't need many words. B-POP Tags celebrated @blackzang_bz as "the king" of Bangladeshi hip hop, the artist "who redefined the hip hop scene in Bangladesh and took it to global heights."

Blackzang and the rise of Bangladeshi hip hop

The caption speaks of celebrating and enjoying "the grace of the king", and closes with two words: "Love and Prayers." Its hashtags, #hiphopbangladesh, #hiphopnation, #bangladeshi and #blackzang, frame the post as a salute from the wider scene, and a reminder of how much of today's Bangla rap owes to the artists who came first.

The cover

The graphic matches the message. A portrait of Blackzang in sunglasses and a shearling-collared jacket sits against a pop-art backdrop of blue and yellow stars, with a gold, spray-painted crown drawn above his head.
