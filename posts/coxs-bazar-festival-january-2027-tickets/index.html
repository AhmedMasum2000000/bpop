Cox's Bazar Festival, 29-30 January 2027: Tier 2 Passes

Two days by the sea at the end of January. Here's what the post says about Tier 2 passes, and what it leaves out.

January 29 and 30 are already circled on a lot of calendars. A two-day festival in Cox's Bazar is selling its Mid Sale Tier 2 passes, and the post says that tier is "selling fast". Here's what we know about these Cox's Bazar festival tickets.

Cox's Bazar festival tickets: Tier 2 prices

  • 2 Day Multi Pass: ৳14,000
  • VIP Pass: ৳24,000

The post urges fans to "secure your place before the next tier arrives" and signs off with "See you at Cox's Bazar", plus a crab and a sunset to set the beach mood.

What the graphic shows

The festival isn't named in the caption or on the cover, so we won't guess. The artwork is a warm, orange-toned collage: two women in sunglasses, a raised glass, and the edge of a DJ controller in the bottom corner. The lineup, venue and where to buy aren't in this post, so look for the official announcement before you book travel.

More on the radar

For more dates, browse Events & Radar or our September events list. If the rave side of the scene is your thing, read about OMDG and Bangladesh's rave revolution and the electric rise of Bangladesh's sound. Everything else lives on the B-POP Tags home page.

FAQ প্রশ্নোত্তর

When is the Cox's Bazar festival?

The post gives the dates as January 29 and 30, with the event taking place in Cox's Bazar.

How much are Tier 2 passes?

In the Mid Sale Tier 2, the 2 Day Multi Pass costs ৳14,000 and the VIP Pass costs ৳24,000. The post says the tier is selling fast and a next tier will follow.
