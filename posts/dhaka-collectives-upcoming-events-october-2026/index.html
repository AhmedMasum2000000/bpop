Eight Dhaka collectives, one question: which upcoming event are you most excited for? Here's who was on the B-POP radar in October 2026.

Looking for upcoming events in Dhaka? On 18 October 2026 B-POP Tags gathered eight Dhaka collectives and organisers with something coming up and asked followers one question: "Which one are you most excited for?"

The Dhaka collectives on our October radar

The post tagged these accounts:

  • @gyanjam_collective
  • @breakfastclub.dhaka
  • @hxtch.me
  • @returntosanctuary
  • @no.cturna.l
  • @nightwavebd
  • @sapphire.dacca
  • @aakaarpencil

Each one is its own crew or creator, and the carousel gives each a slide. Follow them directly for current details, because times and venues for independent events can change at short notice.

If you're new to these names, the simplest approach is to follow all eight, then watch their stories in the days before an event. That's usually where final details, line-ups and last-minute changes show up first. And answer the caption's question in the comments: telling us which one you're most excited for helps us decide who to cover next.

Gyanjam's যা তা! at Jatra Biroti

The first slide belongs to Gyanjam and its event যা তা! (Ja Ta!). The poster lists Jatra Biroti as the venue, with the date and time given as 23 October, 5 PM. It's loud artwork: the title repeated three times in Bangla type over swirls of blue and yellow, a scrawled white figure in the middle, and the Gyanjam name in a spiky logo across the top.

Why collectives drive Dhaka's scene

A lot of the city's alternative music, art and nightlife is put together by small groups rather than large venues. They book the rooms, make the posters and build the crowd, which is why we follow them as closely as the artists. Breakfast Club has come up on our radar before: it's one of the hashtags on our OMDG feature.

For more of what's coming, see our Off the Grid, On Our Radar preview. For how the week turned out, read the 23 October roundup. Everything we cover on going out lives on our events and radar hub, and the rest of the magazine is on the B-POP Tags home page.
