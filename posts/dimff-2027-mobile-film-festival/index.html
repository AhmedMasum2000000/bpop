Requirements? JUST SHOW UP!!!
An open door mobile film fest in 2027 brought to you by @dimff.ulab and @officialulab .

5 Categories. 1 Global Stage. Endless Stories.
📂 Categories:
🚪 Open Door (Mobile/Camera)
📱 Vertical Film (Mobile/Camera, Vertical Format)
🎥 Short Film (Mobile/Camera)
⏱️ One Minute Film (Mobile Only)
🌍 Citizen Journalism (Mobile Only)
Whether you’re a student or a filmmaker from anywhere in the world—this is your chance to tell your story using the power of mobile filmmaking.
📅 Deadline: September 24, 2026
🌐 Submit via FilmFreeway: https://filmfreeway.com/DIMFF
Lights. Camera. Mobile.
Let your story be seen.
