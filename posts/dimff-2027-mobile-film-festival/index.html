DIMFF 2027: Dhaka's Open Door Mobile Film Festival

Requirements? Just show up. DIMFF 2027 opens its doors to students and filmmakers anywhere with five mobile-first categories.

"Requirements? Just show up!" That's the pitch for the DIMFF 2027 mobile film festival, an open door festival brought to you by @dimff.ulab and @officialulab. If you have a phone and a story, this is one of the most accessible film calls we've seen.

DIMFF 2027 mobile film festival categories

"5 Categories. 1 Global Stage. Endless Stories." Here's the line-up:

  • Open Door: mobile or camera
  • Vertical Film: mobile or camera, vertical format
  • Short Film: mobile or camera
  • One Minute Film: mobile only
  • Citizen Journalism: mobile only

Three categories accept work shot on either a mobile or a camera, while One Minute Film and Citizen Journalism are mobile only. Vertical Film is the one to watch if you already shoot in the vertical format your phone defaults to for social video.

Who can submit

The call is open to students and to filmmakers "from anywhere in the world". The aim is to tell your story "using the power of mobile filmmaking", or as the post sums it up: "Lights. Camera. Mobile. Let your story be seen."

Deadline and how to submit to the mobile film festival

  • Deadline: 24 September 2026
  • Submit via: DIMFF on FilmFreeway
  • Website: dimff.net

You can also follow the festival on LinkedIn and YouTube, where the channel name spells DIMFF out as the Dhaka International Mobile Film Festival.

The cover sets the tone: the DIMFF letters filled with bright floral patterns, an illustrated face peeking over them, "Mobile Film" in large type below, and the University of Liberal Arts Bangladesh crest in the corner.

FAQ প্রশ্নোত্তর

What is the DIMFF 2027 submission deadline?

24 September 2026. Films are submitted via FilmFreeway at filmfreeway.com/DIMFF.

What are the DIMFF 2027 categories?

Open Door (mobile/camera), Vertical Film (mobile/camera, vertical format), Short Film (mobile/camera), One Minute Film (mobile only) and Citizen Journalism (mobile only).

Who can enter DIMFF 2027?

The post says the call is open to students and filmmakers from anywhere in the world, with the only requirement being to "just show up".
