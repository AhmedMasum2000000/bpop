One line, one image: B-POP Tags invites you to witness the electric rise of Bangladesh's sound.

"Witness the ELECTRIC rise of Bangladesh." Posted on the same day as our very first post, this one-liner and a pair of headphones set out what B-POP Tags would spend its time on: the Bangladesh electronic music scene and the people pushing it forward.

A snapshot of the Bangladesh electronic music scene

The cover does the talking. Beneath the pink B-POP Tags logo is a photo washed in violet and molten orange: a mirror ball hanging from the ceiling, stage lights blazing, beams of light cutting across the floor, and a few figures in the haze. It's the kind of room many people in Dhaka's independent music scene will recognise, the small, self-organised spaces where much of the new sound has grown.

Follow the current

Our Music & Rave section tracks that rise in detail. Start with OMDG and the genesis of Bangladesh's rave revolution, relive DJ Amit Kirti's set inside the Metro Rail, and read how DJ Shimmerie keeps Dhaka's underground parties going. For where it all started, see The Beginning, or explore everything from the B-POP Tags home page.
