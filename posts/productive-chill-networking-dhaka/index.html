NETWORKING FOR PEOPLE WHO DON'T LIKE NETWORKING 🛜 ARE YOU READY?

Productive Chill is a space for young professionals and creatives to experiment, share and critique ideas or projects.

They can be ongoing, complete or unsuccessful. If you've learned something from the process we want to hear from you. If you're stuck in the process, you might benefit from applying to join a session.

Whether you're a startup bro, singer-songwriter, fashion designer, writer or pediatrician—if you love what you do, want to figure out the thing you want to do, or find the right people to help you do it, come along!

Without REGISTRATION and CONFIRMATION, there will be no entry. Fill out the application with the LINK IN BIO. Or, here's the URL here:

https://forms.gle/ASzR1SEBQ39NpbUz7
