This week the radar turns the question around: instead of asking what you missed, B-POP Tags asks the Dhaka community what slipped past us.

This Dhaka scene roundup from 23 October 2026 flips the usual question. Instead of asking readers which event they missed, B-POP Tags asked: "Which one did we miss?" The post belongs to our weekly radar, where we collect what's been happening across Dhaka's events scene and invite the community to fill in the gaps.

What the 23 October Dhaka scene roundup looks like

The cover keeps the series' visual language: the B-POP Tags logo, a huge "DHAKA" headline running behind an illustrated figure in a yellow cat-eared helmet, and a night skyline glowing red behind it. The caption is short on purpose, "COMMENT DOWN BELOW", because the comment section is where this edition gets finished.

Why we ask the community

No single page can catch every gig, pop-up and gallery night in a city this busy. A lot of the best things in Dhaka are run by small, self-organised crews that announce late and fill up fast. Asking readers to point out what we skipped keeps the radar honest, and it's often how we find the people we feature next.

To see what was on our list going into the week, read Dhaka collectives to watch in October 2026, which included Gyanjam's event on 23 October, or the earlier Off the Grid, On Our Radar preview. The previous edition of this series is the 6 October roundup.

There's more from the scene on the B-POP Tags home page.
