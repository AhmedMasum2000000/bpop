Feel the shimmer: B-POP Tags names DJ Shimmerie as the heart of Dhaka's underground parties, and says it isn't stopping soon.

"Feel the shimmer." Our post on @dj_shimmerie is short, loud and confident: the heart of Dhaka's underground parties "ain't stopping soon!"

DJ Shimmerie and Dhaka underground parties

The caption puts DJ Shimmerie at the centre of the city's underground party circuit and adds a shout-out: "@monsoon.beatz hit the jackpot". The hashtags, #djshimmerie, #dhakarave and #raveculture, make the setting clear.

The visual

The cover is a burst of colour. A figure in a glittering, mirrored face mask and a patterned headscarf throws up a hand sign in front of a glitchy rainbow test-card backdrop, while a glossy purple-and-gold ribbon curls overhead. Fragments of text peek out from behind, but the post lets the energy do the talking. It's a portrait of a scene that dresses up, masks up and keeps moving.
