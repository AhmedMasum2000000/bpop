Dhaka's Vinyl and Cassette Revival

ঢাকায় ফিরছে ভিনাইল আর ক্যাসেটের দিন

পড়ুন

  • VIEW POST ON INSTAGRAM ↗
  • ← HOME

Dhaka is rediscovering a forgotten memory, one record and tape at a time. Here's who B-POP Tags thanked for it.

"Dhaka is enjoying rejuvenation of the forgotten memory." With that line, B-POP Tags marked a shift many collectors will recognise: the return of vinyl and cassettes in Dhaka, and the people helping it happen.

Who's bringing back vinyl and cassettes in Dhaka

The post thanks four accounts by name, @vinylmadnessbd, @saadimhuq, @sursaptakvinyls and @cassettebangla, "and many more". Then it asks the question: "NOW ARE YOU READY?" It's an invitation as much as a thank-you, aimed at anyone who hasn't yet held a record or a tape.

Analog on the cover

The graphic is a love letter to physical formats. A tall stack of Maxell cassettes carries hand-drawn, anime-style artwork across the cases, a figure in sunglasses wears headphones around his neck, and below them colourful discs drift across a bright blue sky.

Go deeper

Looking for where to start digging? Our guides to physical media shops in Dhaka, part 1 and part 2, are the natural next stop. And if your love of physical media extends to print, read about Jamil's Comics and Bangladesh's comic book culture.

More analog stories live in Pop Culture & Analog and on the B-POP Tags home page.
