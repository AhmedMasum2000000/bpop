Another week in Dhaka's underground, another radar check-in. Which one did you miss?

Our Dhaka scene roundup for 6 October 2026 asks the question this series is built around: "Which one did you miss?" It's part of B-POP Tags' weekly radar, a quick look back at what happened across Dhaka's events scene and an open invitation to talk about it.

Inside the 6 October Dhaka scene roundup

The caption is just that one question; the details live on the slides of the original post. The cover follows the series' style: the B-POP Tags logo over a dark red gradient, the word "DHAKA" in giant white letters, and two illustrated women in front of it, one tipping her sunglasses with a red-gloved hand and one in a red jacket and choker.

How to use the weekly radar

Think of each roundup as a checklist. Swipe through on Instagram, note the crews and venues you didn't know, and follow them so you catch the next one. If something you went to isn't there, say so in the comments. That's how the radar grows.

This edition came a few days after our Off the Grid, On Our Radar preview. For earlier and later weeks, see the 22 September roundup and the 23 October roundup. The full archive is on the B-POP Tags home page.
