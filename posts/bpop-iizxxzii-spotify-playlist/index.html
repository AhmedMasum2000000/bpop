Gatekeeping was never an option. Volume 1 of the B-POP x IIZXXZII playlist is out now on Spotify.

The B-POP x IIZXXZII playlist is out, and the reason for it fits in one line: "Gatekeeping was never an option." On 12 September 2026 B-POP Tags shared a Spotify playlist curated with IIZXXZII, "cooked up with raw Serotonin and illegal levels of main-character energy."

Listen to the B-POP x IIZXXZII playlist

You can open it directly on Spotify. The cover announces "VOLUME 1 IS OUT NOW" and shows the playlist art as a CD in a shrink-wrapped jewel case. A close-up of a woman's eyes and bindi sits across a band of navy on green, labelled "VOLUME I", with the B-POP Tags and IIZXXZII names in the corners.

Why a playlist, and why share it

Calling it "Volume 1" suggests more to come. The spirit of the post is the opposite of hoarding finds. A lot of music culture treats discovery as a status thing, where the fewer people who know a track, the better. B-POP Tags started from the other side: if something is good, pass it on. You can read more about that thinking in The Beginning: Why B-POP Tags Exists.

If the playlist leaves you wanting more, start with our features on Mass $cott and modern Bangla trap and Qinetic Music. There's plenty more on our music hub and the B-POP Tags home page.
