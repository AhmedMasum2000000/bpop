An incredible opportunity for Bangladeshi Producers and DJ's.
Send a mix, a bio, a photo and your set goes out from Hong Kong to listeners worldwide, free.

A radio show gives you what promoters, press and visa applications all look for: proof that your work is heard internationally. That’s the barrier that keeps Bangladeshi artists off stages abroad.

Follow @blu.maron and @subjecttosound and comment 'IN' on their post which is attached below, and they’ll DM you the application link.
Not a DJ? Share this.
