Hong Kong Radio Show Opportunity for Bangladeshi DJs and Producers

বাংলাদেশি ডিজে ও প্রডিউসারদের জন্য হংকং রেডিও শোয়ের সুযোগ

Send a mix, a bio and a photo, and your set can go out from Hong Kong to listeners worldwide, for free. Here's the opportunity and how to apply.

Here's an opportunity for Bangladeshi DJs and producers: send in a mix, a bio and a photo, and your set can be broadcast from Hong Kong to listeners around the world, free of charge. B-POP Tags shared the call on 9 October 2026, and it's worth a look if you make or play electronic music in Bangladesh.

What the Hong Kong radio show offers

From our post:

  • You submit three things: a mix, a bio and a photo.
  • Your set goes out from Hong Kong to listeners worldwide.
  • There's no cost to take part.

Our cover summed it up in one line: a mix "broadcasted live from Hong Kong", set over a crowd with their hands in the air.

Why airplay matters for Bangladeshi DJs and producers

The caption was direct about the stakes. A radio show gives you what promoters, press and visa applications all look for: proof that your work is heard internationally. As we put it, that's "the barrier that keeps Bangladeshi artists off stages abroad."

A broadcast credit won't solve everything, but it's concrete and easy to check, and it fits neatly into a press kit, a booking email or an application. For artists building careers from Dhaka, Chattogram or anywhere else in the country, that kind of evidence can be hard to come by.

How to apply

  • Follow @blu.maron and @subjecttosound on Instagram.
  • Find their post (it's attached to ours) and comment "IN".
  • They'll DM you the application link.

Our post didn't include deadlines or selection criteria, so we haven't listed any here. Check with the organisers directly, and have your mix, bio and photo ready before you comment so you can apply as soon as the link lands in your inbox. Not a DJ? The caption asks you to share it with someone who is.
