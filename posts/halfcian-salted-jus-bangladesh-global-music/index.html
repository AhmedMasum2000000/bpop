A big thank-you to Halfcian of Salted Jus for elevating Bangladesh on the global music stage, and a hope for more live shows back home.

Big thanks, in capital letters, to Halfcian of Salted Jus. B-POP Tags dedicated this post to @halfcian, also known as @sahriarisdead, from @saltedjus, for "making Bangladesh elevated in the global stage of music industry."

Halfcian of Salted Jus and Bangladesh's global sound

The caption ends with a wish many fans will share: more live shows and tours "like the old times" when he returns to the country. It signs off by calling him "our golden boy". The hashtags, #ambientmusic, #saltedjus, #bangladeshimusic and #banglapop, sketch the sonic territory, from ambient textures to Bangla pop.

The cover

The graphic is pure B-POP: "WE FOUND ART" in huge hot-pink letters on white, with a strip of photo peeking in at the bottom edge.

More artists going global
