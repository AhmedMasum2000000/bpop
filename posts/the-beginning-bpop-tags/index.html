The Beginning. হইলো শুরু। The first post from B-POP Tags, and the start of everything that followed.

Two lines, two languages: "The Beginning. হইলো শুরু।" That's how B-POP Tags, short for Bangla Pop Tags, opened its account, and it's where this archive starts.

What is B-POP Tags?

B-POP Tags is a magazine that curates Bangladesh's pop culture and underground scene: the DJs and rappers, the artists and designers, the collectors, the film talks and the events you shouldn't miss. Every Instagram post now gets its own page on this site, so you can find the stories again long after they've slipped down the feed.

The first cover

The opening graphic sets the tone. The pink B-POP logo stretches across a bright blue sky full of clouds, while an illustrated woman in heart-shaped pink glasses raises a henna-patterned hand toward the viewer, rings on her fingers and a bindi on her forehead. A postage-stamp detail in the corner adds a collector's touch.

Where to go next

Start with Pop Culture & Analog, then follow the very next post, the electric rise of Bangladesh's sound. Our tribute to Bibi Russell explains why we honour the people who built the culture, and Jamil's Comics shows what that looks like in practice. Or browse everything from the B-POP Tags home page.
