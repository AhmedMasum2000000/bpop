DJ Amit Kirti's DJ Set Inside the Bangladesh Metro Rail

Did you know? Back in 2024, DJ Amit Kirti took a live DJ set inside the Bangladesh Metro Rail.

Did you know? Back in 2024, @djamitkirti played a DJ set in the Dhaka Metro Rail, or as the caption puts it, the Bangladesh Metrorail. Our verdict: he "definitely knew the vision". HARD!

DJ Amit Kirti's DJ set in the Dhaka Metro Rail

B-POP Tags' graphic spells it out: "DJ Amit Kirti did the first ever live DJ set inside the Metro Rail back in April 2024." It shows the DJ in dark sunglasses, gesturing toward the camera, above a green-and-red Metro Rail train, with a prompt to "swipe to see the snippet". The hashtags, #dhaka, #djset, #metrorail and #banglarave, say the rest. Two years on, it's still one of those moments people bring up when they talk about how far the scene will go to find a stage.

Why it still resonates

A set on a train carriage is a reminder that the scene finds stages wherever it can, a spirit that runs through the story of OMDG and Bangladesh's rave revolution. For more of the city's party energy, see DJ Shimmerie and Dhaka's underground parties and the electric rise of Bangladesh's sound.
