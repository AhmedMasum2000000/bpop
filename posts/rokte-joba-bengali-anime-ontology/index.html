rokte joba: anime ontology & bengali post mysticism

for the symposium of post-mysticism at @solo___show (21st-23rd) august, an offsite multimedia showcase and lecture by @aashnai__

drawing from her manga practice, she will be sharing the construction of her pictorial world through the theological, iconographic and affective traditions whose social conditions no longer feel inhabitable. on circulating images in the post-liturgical land, enchanted modernities and how the devotional image sustains fractures.

rokte joba is an ongoing image practice and philosophical investigation into the bengali anime ontology.

along with:

jamie sutcliffe + zoe taylor, meltdown your books, elizabeth gabrielle lee, jacob canet-gibson, ojitosdeemoji, nina, yubel, safi, V, nkieve, denpa archivist, knightmaid, yudi wu, vincent, et aliae, saffron-p, carla martin // september: phoenix isla kea, chrysanthi, mirror_doll, archive_doll, evah, sofia, REM, rahul hendriksen, cali constanza, shubi, daniel
