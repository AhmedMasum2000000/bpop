Mass $cott and the Sound of Modern Bangla Trap

মাস $কট এবং আধুনিক বাংলা ট্র্যাপের সাউন্ড

পড়ুন

  • VIEW POST ON INSTAGRAM ↗
  • ← HOME

If you don't know MASS $COTT yet, consider this your late pass to one of the artists shaping modern Bangla trap.

If you're looking for modern Bangla trap, B-POP Tags has a name for you: MASS $COTT. Our post on 4 October 2026 put it plainly: "If you don't know MASS $COTT yet, consider this your late pass."

Mass $cott's Bangla trap, from "DHAKA OPS" to "Lunch In Dhaka"

The caption points to two tracks as the way in, "DHAKA OPS" and "Lunch In Dhaka", and credits him with "shaping the sound of modern Bangla Trap." Both titles put the city right in the name, which tells you where the music is coming from before you've pressed play.

The cover is soaked in red: a figure with long curls gripping a microphone, set against a halftone swirl, a crosshair and a blade. It's an image that suits the genre's aggression.

Where trap fits in Bangladesh's music

Trap and hip hop have become a big part of how a younger generation in Bangladesh tells its own stories in Bangla. If you want the longer history, read our piece on Blackzang and Bangladeshi hip hop. For more listening, the B-POP x IIZXXZII playlist collects music we're excited about, and our Fiquzic early access post introduces another name to watch.

More artists are on our music hub and the B-POP Tags home page.
