Lunith.Void: Pushing Boundaries in Dhaka's Psychedelic Culture

লুনিথ.ভয়েড: ঢাকার সাইকেডেলিক সংস্কৃতির সীমানা ভাঙা শিল্পী

পড়ুন

  • VIEW POST ON INSTAGRAM ↗
  • ← HOME

From making history to making art for Psydrip, Raiyan's work carries the love-and-unity spirit of the psychedelic scene.

"Pushing boundaries is what Raiyan does best." That's how B-POP Tags introduced @lunith.void, an artist whose work, as our caption put it, represents "the psychedelic culture of love and unity". If you're looking for psychedelic art in Dhaka, he's a name to know.

Psychedelic art in Dhaka's rave community

The caption credits Raiyan with everything "from making history to creating art" for @psydrip.dhk, and signs off with thanks and a reminder to "keep raving hard". Tagged #dhakagram and #psy, the post places his work in the city's psychedelic and rave circles.

A glimpse of his art

The carousel is labelled "Collected via IG" and "Glimpse of his art". Its preview frame shows a collage where a portrait is layered over a newspaper page printed in Arabic script, framed in hot pinks and reds. Above it sits an illustrated eye cradled by two hands in purple and green, a fitting motif for the subject.

Find more visual artists in Art & Fashion. For the sound that goes with the visuals, see the Omnigroove DJ workshop with Psytaraa, DJ Shimmerie and Dhaka's underground parties and OMDG's role in Bangladesh's rave revolution. More on the B-POP Tags home page.
