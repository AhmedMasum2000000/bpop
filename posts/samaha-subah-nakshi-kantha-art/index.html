Samaha Subah: Bangladeshi Heritage Setting International Trends

সামাহা সুবাহ: নকশিকাঁথার ঐতিহ্যে আন্তর্জাতিক ট্রেন্ড

পড়ুন

  • VIEW POST ON INSTAGRAM ↗
  • ← HOME

A short thank-you with a big idea behind it: Samaha Subah's art is one reason Bangladeshi heritage is turning up in international trends.

Some of our posts are simple thank-you notes, and this is one of them. B-POP Tags shared a tribute to Samaha Subah (@samaha1507), crediting her work via @siz.by.ss and @rejectproject.shop and tagging it #nakshikantha, a nod to nakshi kantha art and the Bangladeshi heritage it carries.

Nakshi kantha art through a new lens

The caption makes one clear point: because of innovators like her, "Bangladeshi heritage is setting international trends". The cover graphic backs that up. Under a bold "WE FOUND ART" headline, a black-and-white portrait of the artist sits beside a dark panel of dashed, stitch-like lines arranged in diamonds and circles, a pattern that recalls the running stitch of a kantha. The other hashtags, #bangladeshrural and #arka, point the same way: back to rural craft, forward to contemporary design.

Why we're paying attention

Heritage carried into new contexts is a recurring thread in our Art & Fashion coverage. It links this post to our tribute to Bibi Russell, to the sharper, futuristic edge of cybersigilism in Bangladeshi fashion, and to artists like Hridita Anisha, who build culture rather than decorate it.

Want to see more of Samaha Subah's work? Start with the accounts above, then find more artist spotlights on the B-POP Tags home page.
