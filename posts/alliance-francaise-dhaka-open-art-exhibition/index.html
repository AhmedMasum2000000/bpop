Personal memory, selfhood, lived experience and unspoken narratives: the Open to All exhibition at La Galerie, Alliance Française de Dhaka, ran 1–9 September 2026.

For anyone searching for an art exhibition in Dhaka in early September 2026, B-POP Tags flagged one with an open door: the Open to All Art Exhibition at La Galerie, Alliance Française de Dhaka. It was presented by @afdhaka and curated by @zeba_dhaka.

About the Open to All art exhibition in Dhaka

Our caption described the show as a varied collection of pieces investigating the deep connections between "personal recollection, selfhood, lived experience, and unspoken narratives." That's a broad brief, and a generous one. It invites work about the private stories people carry but rarely say out loud. Any one of those four ideas could carry a show by itself; putting them together lets one person's memory sit beside another's sense of self, so the pieces end up in conversation.

The invitation card on our cover carries the title না বলা কথা (Na Bola Kotha), with "Untold Story" printed in English beneath it, a fitting name for a show about the unspoken. The photo is credited via Lilac Amour on Instagram.

Dates, venue and opening hours

  • Dates: 1–9 September 2026
  • Venue: La Galerie, Alliance Française de Dhaka, 26 Mirpur Road, Dhanmondi, Dhaka
  • Hours: open daily, 3:00–9:00 PM
  • Presented by: Alliance Française de Dhaka
  • Curated by: @zeba_dhaka

The exhibition has now closed. If you went, tell us what stayed with you in the comments on the original post.

Why shows like this matter

Galleries can feel closed off. A show that announces itself as open to all and builds its theme around ordinary memory lowers that barrier. Visitors don't need a background in art to recognise a story about who they are or what went unsaid at home.

Exhibitions like this are a big part of our art and fashion coverage. For an artist working with similar questions of identity and belonging, read our profile of Hridita Anisha.
