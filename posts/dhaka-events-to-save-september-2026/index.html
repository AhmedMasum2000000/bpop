Save this so you don't miss out: B-POP Tags' radar picks for Dhaka events in September 2026.

Planning your month around Dhaka events in September 2026? On 20 September B-POP Tags shared a set of Dhaka events to save, with a practical instruction: "Save this so you don't miss out." The caption also asked the question every good plan starts with: "Which event are you pulling up to?"

How to use this list of Dhaka events to save

The events are on the slides of the original Instagram post, so that's where you'll find names, dates and venues. Save the post to your collection, check back before the weekend, and follow the organisers whose events catch your eye. Independent events in Dhaka often share last-minute changes on their own pages first.

The cover uses our off-the-grid look: an "off the grid / on our radar" line with a red cat logo in the corner, the B-POP Tags wordmark, "DHAKA" in huge letters and an illustrated character with skull hair clips and a spiked collar.

What came next

We followed this post two days later with the 22 September roundup, which asked what readers missed. The previous roundup was on 9 September, and the same off-the-grid format came back for October's events.

All of our event previews and roundups are on the events and radar hub. For the rest of the magazine, head to the B-POP Tags home page.
