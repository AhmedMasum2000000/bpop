Embryonic Shifts: A Stroboscopic STE(A)M Art Installation

এমব্রায়োনিক শিফটস: আলো, ভাস্কর্য আর বিজ্ঞানের স্ট্রোবোস্কোপিক ইনস্টলেশন

Light, sculpture and science move as one in a new installation that uses strobe light to bring still sculptures to life.

Embryonic Shifts is a new stroboscopic art installation that sets out to make still sculptures move. Created by artist Saiqa Shabnam Chowdhury and engineer Samiul Hoque, and supported by the British Council under WOW – Women of the World Bangladesh 2025–26, it's billed as a STE(A)M art installation "where light, sculpture and science move as one".

How the stroboscopic art installation works

The piece uses flashing light and what the announcement calls "the earliest principles of animation". Under the strobe, still sculptures appear to move, much like a sequence of still frames reads as motion on screen. That illusion carries a story of metamorphosis, which the announcement describes as "a phenomenon every living being goes through in some form or the other". Because the effect depends on light and timing, this is the kind of work that rewards seeing it in person rather than on a phone screen.

The idea behind Embryonic Shifts

According to the announcement, the project takes "a clear, empirical stance". It posits that gender identity is an outcome of biological internal systems rather than an endless social debate. It uses persistence of vision to examine human perception through empirical science, with fiction and sequential storytelling as its methods for exploring "structural realities".

The cover keeps things minimal: the title in spaced, elegant capitals beside a butterfly whose wing doubles as a human profile, a neat visual shorthand for transformation.

Venue, opening night and hours

  • Venue: Shala, Aloki
  • Opening night: Saturday, 22 August 2026, 6:00 PM, with remarks and an introduction to the work
  • Exhibition hours: 1:00 PM to 9:00 PM

The post doesn't give a closing date, so check before you go.

JOIN THE EMBRYONIC SHIFTS — a STE(A)M art installation
Where light, sculpture and science move as one.

We're thrilled to announce Embryonic Shifts, a new stroboscopic sculptural installation by artist Saiqa Shabnam Chowdhury and engineer Samiul Hoque, supported by the British Council under WOW – Women of the World Bangladesh 2025–26.
